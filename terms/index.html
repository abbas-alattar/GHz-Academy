You must give accurate information when you create an account. Students must verify their email. Keep your password private and tell us if you think someone else used your account.

Courses

Courses are delivered online. Access to a paid course starts after an academy admin confirms that payment has been received.

Registration and payment

When you register, your request is pending. After you pay, it is marked paid. An admin then confirms the registration, and you are enrolled. Until confirmation, you do not have full course access.
